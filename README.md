## How to Go Live (Free GitHub Pages Hosting)

### Step 1 — Create GitHub Account
Go to https://github.com/join and sign up (free).

### Step 2 — Create Repository
- Click **"New repository"**
- Name it exactly: `mdabdulkaderhemal.github.io`
- Set it to **Public**
- Click **"Create repository"**

### Step 3 — Upload Files
- Click **"uploading an existing file"**
- Drag and drop: `index.html`, `schema.json`, and your `photo.jpg`
- Click **"Commit changes"**

### Step 4 — Enable GitHub Pages
- Go to **Settings** → **Pages**
- Source: **Deploy from a branch** → **main** → **/ (root)**
- Click **Save**

### Step 5 — Your site is live! 🎉
Visit: `https://mdabdulkaderhemal.github.io`
(Takes 2–5 minutes to go live)

## Tell Google About Your Site (After Going Live)

### Google Search Console
1. Go to https://search.google.com/search-console
2. Click **"Add property"** → enter your URL
3. Verify ownership → **Submit**
4. Go to **URL Inspection** → paste your URL → **Request Indexing**

### Google People Card (Do This On Your Phone!)
1. Open Google on your **mobile phone**
2. Search: `Md Abdul Kader Hemal`
