Who I Am

About Me

Hello! I'm Hemal 👋

I am Md Abdul Kader Hemal, a graduate in Electrical and Electronic Engineering (EEE) from the University of Dhaka, Bangladesh. My academic journey has been deeply focused on control systems engineering — particularly Adaptive Control, Fuzzy Logic, and Sliding Mode Control techniques.

My undergraduate thesis investigated the performance and energy-efficiency of multiple advanced control strategies for PMDC Motor Speed Regulation in Electric Vehicles — a cutting-edge area at the intersection of power electronics and sustainable transportation.

I also have hands-on experience in teaching and tutoring at the school and college level, where I enjoy simplifying complex engineering concepts into clear, understandable lessons.
